The Studio

Two people. One process.

Quintilian, Inc. is a two-person studio. Chuck Huber builds the systems; Jessica von Braun-Huber directs how everything looks and reads. We came up on opposite sides of the same convention floor — and we’ve been building from inside the problem ever since. Nothing ships without both sets of eyes.

Chuck Huber

The builder behind the systems.

Chuck builds what Quintilian ships — a convention-booking platform, 867 The Line, a sovereign AI stack on owned hardware, a RAG pipeline across 73 patents. The pattern never changes: find the friction, remove it, build the platform on top.

He came to it honestly. Years inside conventions and guest booking meant living the same broken systems on repeat — the four-hour line worst of all — so he built the fix. And he’s taught the craft for 35 years, which is why the training holds up: we teach what we actually build, from your first prompt to a full production pipeline, in your own stack.

He also spent two decades as an anime voice actor — Android 17, Hiei, Stein. That work lives at chuckhuber.org.

Jessica von Braun-Huber

The eye on everything that ships.

Nearly two decades of art direction and illustration — five years in-house at an agency, a decade on the convention circuit as a cover artist, freelance ever since. Her work hangs from Pop Gallery at Disney Springs and Epcot to the permanent collection at Lambeth Palace in London. Covers for Image Comics and Aftershock; campaigns for Coca-Cola, Papa John’s, Dairy Queen, and McAfee.

She directs generative AI to production quality — every major model pushed to billboard scale, then carried the rest of the way by hand with digital painting, photography, and a finished pass. The fundamentals don’t change: composition, color, type, and story. The toolkit does.

At conventions, she’s the one drawing the characters he voices.

His structures get her pass.

Chuck builds the systems — production-grade, made to run in the real world. Every one gets a creative pass from Jessica before it ships, so it holds up and looks the part.
