About

The eye behind the work.

Quintilian, Inc. is a multidisciplinary creative practice and applied-AI consultancy — two decades of art direction and illustration, brought to the modern table.

An artist her entire life.

Nearly two decades of art direction, illustration, and mixed-media work across publishing, agency, and in-house creative. A working illustrator and freelance cover artist for over a decade on the convention circuit, with work that has hung everywhere from Pop Gallery at Disney Springs and Epcot to Lambeth Palace in London. Five years in-house at an ad agency, then back to freelance.

Illustration, traditional work, digital, graphic design, print, AI integrations, AI imagery, typography, layout, brand development, NFT drops, convention banners — if you need it, she’s probably done it. One of Midjourney’s original beta testers, with an eye that has grown across every branch of AI in the years since.

The fundamentals don’t change — composition, color, type, and story. What’s new is the toolkit. The value isn’t the tool. It’s the eye directing it.

The creative practice.

Brand campaigns, illustration, native iOS apps, NFT collections, and publishing — designed, built, and shipped in-house. Campaigns for Coca-Cola, Papa John’s, and Dairy Queen; cover art for Image Comics, Aftershock, and Blue Angel Publishing; a family of apps built end-to-end from concept through App Store launch.

The applied-AI consultancy.

Quintilian, Inc. is where strategic consulting meets applied AI. We’re not a software house that learned to say “AI,” and we’re not a strategy firm bolting on a technology practice. We’ve built and operated intelligent systems from the ground up — production systems, not proofs of concept.
